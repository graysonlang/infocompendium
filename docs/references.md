# References

- Paul David Doherty, *Infocom Fact Sheet*: <http://pdd.if-legends.org/infocom/fact-sheet.txt>. Section VIII covers the compilations. The Masterpieces subsection is visibly unfinished - it omits Leather Goddesses entirely and has a placeholder in the prose - so prefer disc evidence where the two disagree.
- Andrew Plotkin, *The Obsessively Complete Infocom Catalog*: <https://eblong.com/infocom/>. Per-game file lists with release and serial numbers, and `zcanalyze.py` for reading z-code headers.
- The leaked Infocom ZIL sources: <https://github.com/historicalsource>. Used here for the Zork Zero border analysis.
- Mark Howell's ztools (`txd`, `infodump`, `pix2gif`): <https://ifarchive.org/indexes/if-archive/infocom/tools/ztools/>. Fetched and built by `scripts/get-ztools.sh`.
- ZCut: <https://ifarchive.org/if-archive/infocom/tools/zcut11.c>. Extracts a story file from an Apple II, Atari 8-bit, Commodore 64/128 or Amstrad CPC disk image, walking each platform's on-disk layout rather than assuming the story is contiguous. It is the practical way to get a story file off original 8-bit media, and it reports the interpreter letter as a bonus. Two cautions: it needs C99 (`enum tsystem` is forward-declared through a typedef and wants a small edit to build), and its "Checksum good" is the Z-machine's 16-bit byte sum, which passes on a permuted image - verify the result independently.
- Greaseweazle: <https://github.com/keirf/greaseweazle>. Flux-level floppy reader used for every diskette in this catalog; its [Flippy Disks](https://github.com/keirf/greaseweazle/wiki/Flippy-Disks) wiki page covers reading second surfaces, and issues [#460](https://github.com/keirf/greaseweazle/issues/460) (Commodore) and [#473](https://github.com/keirf/greaseweazle/issues/473) (Apple II) document why a flippy's reverse side reads backwards.
- IF Archive Infocom media: <https://ifarchive.org/indexes/if-archive/infocom/media/>. Portable conversions of the Lurking Horror and Sherlock sound files.
- Redump: <http://redump.org/>. This repo's pressings of Masterpieces (disc 64685) and The Zork Anthology (disc 82144) hash-match their entries; Redump's Infocom coverage is otherwise thin - the Lost Treasures volume 1 CDs appear absent entirely.
